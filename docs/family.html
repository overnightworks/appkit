3 · Sign-in page

Family

The three states, the same structure and the same words in every app; only the colours are the app's. These follow the page theme above. The faint grid is --glow-primary, drawn here in the app's colour; at 3 to 4 % it is barely visible either way.

Each state has one way out: “Log in”, “Log out”, “Try again”. The page never redirects by itself. Words: the ruled sign-in list (en and de) in the kit's stylesheet reference.

4 · Account menu

Family

The circle opens one menu, the same in every app: your name (opens the central account, ↗), the theme, “Log out”, and the quiet legal row. These follow the page theme above.

The theme choice belongs to one person in one app. An app that cannot store it per person shows no theme rows and follows the device. An app adds its own rows above Theme (songmaker: Settings).

A name a person typed keeps its case, also in the uppercase headings; the words the app wrote stay uppercase. presentator's source “talks” and the menu's name show it.
